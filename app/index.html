A VISUAL LANGUAGE / FROM LINGUISTICS TO FORM

Learn to read the DNA.

Every channel has a meaning. Change one and watch the same exchange respond.

Only beads mark spoken contributions. Backbones track speaker identity.
↓

Backbone → sequence organization

Vertical position preserves source turn order. Following a strand keeps one participant visible through an interleaved exchange.

Order is observed; elapsed time is not inferred.
⌬

Bases → communicative moves

CLQ, ANS, and ACK expose clarification, answer, and acknowledgement. Native labels remain available beside the transcript.

↗

Pairings → sequential relevance

A directed link connects a contribution to its annotated target, even across intervening turns. Pairings expose what a response is attached to.

Attachment does not establish successful understanding.
≋

Twist → participation rhythm

Local speaker switching tightens the helix. Runs from one speaker create a looser turn. Fixed pitch gives a neutral reference view.

↔

Radius → response reach

Wider turns mark a response reaching farther back in source order. Delayed answers become visible without being mistaken for adjacent ones.

●

Bead size → contribution length

Beads grow with whitespace-delimited word count, up to a size cap. This measures length, not linguistic complexity, importance, or authority.

⋯

Texture → what is actually known

Solid links are annotated or platform targets. Dashed links are chronology only. UNK preserves unknown actions.

A missing annotation is not evidence that a person was ignored.

PROVENANCE & METHODS

An atlas, not a theory of people.

The DNA metaphor encodes conversational moves, speaker strands, and response links. It does not imply biological inheritance, a universal grammar of interaction, or a measure of personal worth.

Created by Baihan Lin, PhD · doerlbh@gmail.com

What the matching score means

A structural index proposes up to 120 candidate episodes. Local sequence alignments are reranked by typed-edge correspondence and speaker identity relationships. The score is an engineering ranking signal, not a probability or a measure of success. Unknown actions remain unknown; no language model silently annotates this index.

Annotation evidence is not interchangeable

Molweni provides human discourse annotations. Reddit provides majority-vote labels and platform reply links. Chromium and ChangeMyView provide platform links. DeliData, DailyDialog, WildChat, and PRISM have chronological scaffolds or observed prompt-response pairs, which are never scored as inferred semantic targets. DailyDialog speaker lanes assume alternation. Source inventories retain their original labels.

Limits of the collection

WildChat is one non-toxic-filtered shard with exact transcript deduplication; this is not the full WildChat corpus. DailyDialog uses its training split. DeliData includes MESSAGE rows only. PRISM follows the unique selected path and stops at ambiguous selection. Chromium is large but mostly short. Local raw texts may contain sensitive material; the source licenses and access conditions still apply.

Related work

WildVis supports large-scale content exploration. ConDynS compares conversational dynamics through summaries and patterns. This atlas adds an explicit local graph inspection workflow; its benchmarks do not establish general superiority over those systems.
